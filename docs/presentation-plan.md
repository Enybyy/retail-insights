# Presentation plan

Retail Insights is a reproducible retail sales study for hiring clients and commercial analysts. Its opening view should show a real result and let a reader trace it to the underlying analysis.

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | #FFFFFF | Report background |
| Ink | #16324F | Headings and primary text |
| Slate | #526477 | Explanations and axis labels |
| Petrol | #087F8C | Merchandise purchase series |
| Amber | #C07A2C | Partial periods and exceptions |
| Mist | #E8EEF3 | Table separators and subtle surfaces |

Use Segoe UI for the report and system sans-serif for exported charts. Left-aligned prose, a compact contents rail on wide screens and full-width figures within the reading column. A single analytical chart opens the report; findings follow in prose rather than decorative metric tiles.

```text
Contents rail | Title and scope
              | Monthly sales chart
              | Findings and decision implications
              | Audit -> sales -> customers -> recommended tests
              | Methods and reproducibility
```

The main visual distinction is the connection between commercial findings and ledger treatments. The amber partial-month column is information, not decoration. Mobile tables scroll within their container.
